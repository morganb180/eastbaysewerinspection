# How we verify listings

This is a guide site with no contractor listings yet. Program facts come from EBMUD (ebmud.com) and the Regional PSL site (eastbaypsl.com), read on 2026-10-12, plus the City of Berkeley's program pages.

## What we check

* Each city page states its source and date.
* No contractor is listed until it can be checked against its California (CSLB) license record.

## What we don't verify

* Workmanship, prices, insurance or availability.
* Phone numbers and websites added later by a provider, until the claim process checks them against the official record.

## Corrections

Spot an error? Use [list, claim, correct or remove a listing](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/list-your-business/). Every request will be checked against the official credential record before anything changes. See also [about our data](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/about-our-data/).
