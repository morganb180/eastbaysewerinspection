# About our data

* **Program facts:** [EBMUD PSL page](https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/private-sewer-laterals) and [eastbaypsl.com](https://www.eastbaypsl.com/), read 2026-10-12. Public agency pages, with no reuse license stated.
* **Contractor list:** EBMUD's courtesy list, the [EBMUD PSL Program Contractor List](https://www.eastbaypsl.com/doc/ContractorList.pdf) (FY27, dated August 2026), downloaded 2026-10-30. EBMUD calls it a courtesy resource: contractors volunteered to be on it and self-certified the services they offer; EBMUD does not endorse them and the list is not exclusive. Public agency document, with no reuse license stated. We publish contractors as printed (name, address, phone, email, services); the PDF itself stays on EBMUD's site at the link above.
* **License status:** each contractor's license number was matched to the [CSLB License Master file](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/dataportal/ContractorList) (CSLB public data portal, data as of 2026-10-28, downloaded 2026-10-30): 41 active (CLEAR) and listed; 2 showed a suspension and are not listed. Where CSLB's business name differs from EBMUD's, the card shows both. Status can change daily; check CSLB's [Instant License Check](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx) before hiring.
* **Fees:** [EBMUD PSL Program Fees](https://www.eastbaypsl.com/fees.html) (effective July 1, 2026), checked 2026-10-13.
* **Berkeley:** [city PSL page](https://berkeleyca.gov/city-services/streets-sidewalks-sewers-and-utilities/private-sewer-lateral), [BMC 17.24.080](https://berkeley.municipal.codes/BMC/17.24.080), found via search 2026-10-12.
