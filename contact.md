# Contact East Bay Sewer Inspection

East Bay Sewer Inspection is a small independent directory and guide. We read every message. We aren't a contractor, inspector or government agency, and we can't do sewer lateral work ourselves.

## Email

[hello@eastbaysewerinspection.com](mailto:hello@eastbaysewerinspection.com): questions, data corrections and advertising. When writing about a specific listing, please include its listing ID (for example your CSLB license number).

## Listings

To claim, correct, add or remove a listing, use the [listing request form](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/list-your-business/).
