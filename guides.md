# East Bay sewer lateral guides

Guides to the EBMUD Regional Private Sewer Lateral program and Berkeley's city program. Each guide links the EBMUD, city or CSLB page it relies on.

- [How the EBMUD sewer lateral program works](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/ebmud-psl-program/): How the EBMUD Regional Private Sewer Lateral (PSL) ordinance works at sale: Compliance Certificate, verification test, time extensions, exemptions.
- [Sewer lateral certificate fees and costs](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/sewer-lateral-cost/): EBMUD sewer lateral certificate fees ($407 Compliance Certificate as of July 2026) and what drives contractor repair and replacement costs.
- [Where the sewer lateral rule applies](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/cities/): Cities and areas covered by the EBMUD Regional Private Sewer Lateral ordinance, with start dates, plus Berkeley's separate program.
- [Contractors on EBMUD's list](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/contractors/): 41 contractors from EBMUD's PSL courtesy list, with services, phone, email and active CSLB licenses, plus how to vet a sewer lateral contractor.
- [Sewer lateral inspection](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/sewer-lateral-inspection/): The contractor camera check, the EBMUD-witnessed verification test, fees and how long certificates last.
- [Sewer lateral replacement cost](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/sewer-lateral-replacement-cost/): What is published about replacement costs, the factors EBMUD says drive a bid, and help paying.

* [Who Is Responsible for the Sewer Lateral? East Bay City Rules](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/sewer-lateral-responsibility/): Who owns which part of the lateral in each city (upper vs lower), the local code that requires upkeep, and the permit office to call.
* [EBMUD Time Extension Certificate: $4,500 Deposit & 180 Days](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/time-extension-certificate/): Closing before the lateral is fixed: the $142 fee, the $4,500 refundable deposit, the 180-day clock and how the refund works.
* [Sewer Lateral Exemptions: Inheritance, Trusts, Divorce (EBMUD)](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/sewer-lateral-exemptions/): Inheritance, living trusts, divorce, family and LLC transfers that don't trigger the certificate, plus septic and no-lateral exemptions.
* [Selling or Buying a Condo: EBMUD Sewer Lateral & HOA Rules](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/condo-hoa-sewer-lateral/): Condo and townhome sales: HOA vs unit owner responsibility, the Statement of Responsibility, and what buyers should ask for.
* [Does This House Have a Sewer Lateral Certificate? How to Check](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/check-sewer-lateral-certificate/): For buyers and owners: look up a property's certificate status, how long certificates last (7 or 20 years), and voluntary certificates.
* [Remodeling Over $100,000? The EBMUD Sewer Lateral Rule](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/remodeling-sewer-lateral-certificate/): Remodeling an existing East Bay home for more than $100,000: when the certificate is due, the steps, and Berkeley's building-permit rule.
