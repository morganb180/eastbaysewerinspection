Sewer lateral replacement cost in the East Bay

Last updated 2026-11-01. Checked against the official EBMUD and city sources listed at the end of this page.

Reviewed by East Bay Sewer Inspection staff

EBMUD and the cities do not publish a standard price for replacing a sewer lateral, because contractors set their own prices. What is published: EBMUD's certificate fee is $407, city permits are extra, and EBMUD lists the factors that change a bid. Get at least three written bids.

What figures are published?

The only official replacement estimate we found is old. In 2018, the City of Oakland estimated that completely replacing a leaky private sewer lateral could cost about $4,000 to $6,000.

That figure comes from Oakland's Sewer Consent Decree FAQ, posted August 31, 2018 (archived copy). The page has since been removed from oaklandca.gov. It is several years old, so treat it as background, not a current quote. Current prices come only from written contractor bids.

Which fees are fixed?

EBMUD's fees are fixed and published. City permit fees vary by city.

EBMUD says that in most cities, repairing or replacing a lateral needs a city sewer lateral permit, and that Emeryville also requires a permit to test as is. Permit fees vary by city. Ask for a copy of the signed-off (finaled) city permit (EBMUD bid guidance).

What changes the price of a replacement?

EBMUD lists lateral length, pipe material, work method, access to the public main, required permits and landscaping as the main factors.

  • Length: depending on the city, the length is measured from where the lateral leaves the building to the public main, or to the curb in Alameda and Albany.
  • Where the main is: the public main may be in the street, an easement, a neighbour's parcel, the sidewalk or under the building. If it is in the street, the bid should include excavating or repaving the street. In Alameda and Albany, you may have to install a cleanout at the property line.
  • Method: the most common methods are open trench and pipe bursting (trenchless). The contractor recommends one based on the site.
  • Pipe material: the most common are HDPE SDR17, cast iron, PVC and clay.
  • Landscaping: the lateral may run under concrete, a porch, lawn or garden, or need rerouting around a tree. Ask for a restoration estimate.
  • Extra items: fixing illicit connections (for example a downspout tied to the sewer), installing or rerouting a sump pump, extra laterals or shared private mains.

Source: EBMUD, Reviewing a Contractor Bid.

Repair or replace?

Your contractor should recommend one of three options: test as is, repair, or full replacement. A full replacement gets a 20-year certificate, while a repair or pass as is gets 7 years.

EBMUD defines a repair as minimal work, such as replacing sections of pipe or couplings or installing cleanouts. A replacement means new pipe for the entire lateral (EBMUD bid guidance). The certificate periods come from EBMUD.

What should a bid include?

Ask whether the bid covers the EBMUD Compliance Certificate fee, city permits, fixing illicit connections and possible rescheduling fees.

For a replacement, EBMUD says the bid should cover the number of laterals, manholes and mains on the parcel, the city permit, the public main location, the work method, pipe length and material, landscape restoration, and how illicit connections will be found and fixed. Ask the contractor for copies of EBMUD receipts (EBMUD bid guidance).

Is there help paying for it?

Berkeley offers interest-free loans to eligible low-income owners, but only where the City has found the lateral to be a public nuisance. The loans are not available for sales or major renovations.

Details and eligibility are on the City of Berkeley's PSL page (Berkeley). In a sale, the buyer and seller negotiate who pays for the certificate and any work (EBMUD).

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to replace a sewer lateral in Oakland?

There is no current official figure. In 2018 the City of Oakland estimated about $4,000 to $6,000 for a complete replacement, but that is several years old. Get written bids from at least three contractors, as EBMUD recommends.

Are EBMUD fees included in a contractor bid?

Not always. EBMUD says the bid should list its fees, including the $407 Compliance Certificate fee, and the contractor should give you copies of EBMUD receipts.

Is trenchless replacement allowed?

Yes. EBMUD names pipe bursting (trenchless) and open trench as the most common methods. The contractor picks the method for the site.

Who pays for the sewer lateral in a home sale?

The buyer and seller negotiate who obtains the Compliance Certificate.
