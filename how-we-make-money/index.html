How we make money

East Bay Sewer Inspection is free to use. Here is how it is, and will be, paid for, and the rules we hold ourselves to.

Today

  • Nothing is for sale. Every listing is free and built from official public records.
  • Quote requests are free for homeowners, and listed businesses don't pay for them today. Each request connects you with up to 3 of the nearest contractors (or the one you picked plus the 2 nearest others). They contact you directly. The router only uses contractors that have an email address on the source list; that address is not shown on the site.
  • Listings are ordered by location and name. No one can pay to appear higher, to get a license badge, or to have a competitor removed.

Planned

  • Featured placements, clearly labeled "Ad", shown alongside, never instead of, the full free list.

Our rules

  • Paid or not, every listing must match a current official record.
  • Guides and cost pages are written independently of advertisers and cite their sources.
  • If we ever earn a referral fee from a link, the link will say so.

Professionals: see claim or correct your listing.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

EBMUD's PSL contractor list (August 2026) and CSLB license records. Not affiliated with EBMUD. A listing is not an endorsement.

How listings are ordered

By the city of the business address, then by name. No one can pay to appear higher.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the CSLB license number and check it on CSLB's Instant License Check. Confirm the parcel with EBMUD (psl@ebmud.com or 866-403-2683).

On EBMUD's contractor list? Your free listing is built from that list and the CSLB record. Claim it to correct the public details.
