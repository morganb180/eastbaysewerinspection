About our data

  • Program facts: EBMUD PSL page and eastbaypsl.com, read 2026-10-12. Public agency pages, with no reuse license stated.
  • Contractor list: EBMUD's courtesy list, the EBMUD PSL Program Contractor List (FY27, dated August 2026), downloaded 2026-10-30. EBMUD calls it a courtesy resource: contractors volunteered to be on it and self-certified the services they offer; EBMUD does not endorse them and the list is not exclusive. Public agency document, with no reuse license stated. We publish the contractor's name, mailing address and self-certified services. Phone, email and website are not shown. A homeowner reaches a contractor through the quote form, which connects them with up to 3 nearby licensed contractors. They contact the homeowner directly. The PDF itself stays on EBMUD's site at the link above.
  • License status: each contractor's license number was matched to the CSLB License Master file (CSLB public data portal, data as of 2026-10-28, downloaded 2026-10-30): 41 active (CLEAR) and listed; 2 showed a suspension and are not listed. Where CSLB's business name differs from EBMUD's, the card shows both. Status can change daily; check CSLB's Instant License Check before hiring.
  • Fees: EBMUD PSL Program Fees (effective July 1, 2026), checked 2026-10-13.
  • Berkeley: city PSL page, BMC 17.24.080, found via search 2026-10-12.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

EBMUD's PSL contractor list (August 2026) and CSLB license records. Not affiliated with EBMUD. A listing is not an endorsement.

How listings are ordered

By the city of the business address, then by name. No one can pay to appear higher.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the CSLB license number and check it on CSLB's Instant License Check. Confirm the parcel with EBMUD (psl@ebmud.com or 866-403-2683).

On EBMUD's contractor list? Your free listing is built from that list and the CSLB record. Claim it to correct the public details.
