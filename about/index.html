About East Bay Sewer Inspection

East Bay Sewer Inspection explains the EBMUD Regional Private Sewer Lateral (PSL) ordinance: when a Compliance Certificate is needed to close escrow in Oakland, Alameda, Albany, Emeryville, Piedmont and the Stege Sanitary District, how the verification test works, and Berkeley's separate program.

It is for East Bay home sellers, buyers, agents and escrow officers. It also lists 41 contractors from EBMUD's PSL contractor list (a courtesy list) whose licenses are active on CSLB records. Contractors can claim or correct a listing on the list-your-business page.

Independence

East Bay Sewer Inspection is independent. It isn't affiliated with any government agency, licensing board or contractor, and a listing is not an endorsement. See how we make money, how we verify listings and about our data.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

EBMUD's PSL contractor list (August 2026) and CSLB license records. Not affiliated with EBMUD. A listing is not an endorsement.

How listings are ordered

By the city of the business address, then by name. No one can pay to appear higher.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the CSLB license number and check it on CSLB's Instant License Check. Confirm the parcel with EBMUD (psl@ebmud.com or 866-403-2683).

On EBMUD's contractor list? Your free listing is built from that list and the CSLB record. Claim it to correct the public details.
