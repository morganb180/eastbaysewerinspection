From the record

The certificate is an EBMUD document.

In the EBMUD cities, a Compliance Certificate is required to close escrow. EBMUD's fee for that certificate is $407 (effective July 1, 2026), separate from the contractor's bid. A CCTV video alone is not the certificate.
