Get free quotes from up to 3 sewer lateral contractors

Tell us what you need and the ZIP code of the property. We send your request to up to 3 sewer lateral contractors on EBMUD's PSL contractor list (active CSLB licence) near you, and email you their names. They contact you directly. It's free, and there's no obligation. Available where the EBMUD sewer lateral rule applies: Oakland, Alameda, Albany, Berkeley, Emeryville, Piedmont, El Cerrito, Kensington and the Richmond Annex.

What do you need?

Selling a home? Choose the first option. Not sure? We'll send it to residential lateral contractors.

How it works

  1. We match your ZIP code to sewer lateral contractors on EBMUD's PSL contractor list (active CSLB licence), closest first. If you picked one, your request goes to it plus the 2 nearest others. Nobody can pay to be picked.
  2. Each one gets your name, contact details, ZIP code, what you need and your note by email.
  3. You get an email listing who we contacted. We keep a copy of your request to run the service and never sell it. We only send you sewer lateral tips and offers if you tick the optional box.

Prefer to choose yourself?

The contractor list shows every company on EBMUD's list with the phone, email or website it publishes. Get written bids from two or three.
