What happens to your info
  • Up to 3 firms receive your name, email address, phone if you gave one, ZIP, the service and any note, so they can contact you directly.
  • They contact you. We don't call or text you.
  • Their names appear on the next screen.
  • We keep a copy of the request to run the service. A shorter routing record (request ID, date, state, first three ZIP digits, service, and who was contacted) is kept about 13 months.
  • We don't sell your details. Tips and offers only if you tick the optional box.
  • Delete our copy anytime: hello@eastbaysewerinspection.com. Firms keep what they received under their own policies.

Prefer to choose yourself? The contractor list shows every company on EBMUD's list with its business address, services and CSLB license number. Phone, email and website are not on the listing.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

EBMUD's PSL contractor list (August 2026) and CSLB license records. Not affiliated with EBMUD. A listing is not an endorsement.

How listings are ordered

By the city of the business address, then by name. No one can pay to appear higher.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the CSLB license number and check it on CSLB's Instant License Check. Confirm the parcel with EBMUD (psl@ebmud.com or 866-403-2683).

On EBMUD's contractor list? Your free listing is built from that list and the CSLB record. Claim it to correct the public details.
