# Finding a sewer lateral contractor

**No listings yet, and we're saying so on purpose.** EBMUD provides a *courtesy list* of contractors ([ContractorList.pdf](https://www.eastbaypsl.com/doc/ContractorList.pdf), linked from [ebmud.com](https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/private-sewer-laterals)). Use that list as a starting point; we only publish names we can verify. Contractors: [list your business](https://eastbaysewerinspection.com/list-your-business/). We'll check your California contractor license (CSLB) before listing.

## How to vet a contractor

* Check their California contractor license on the CSLB website. Lateral work is commonly done by plumbing (C-36) or general engineering (A) contractors.
* Ask whether they schedule and set up the EBMUD verification test and pull the city permit.
* Get the CCTV video and a written scope: repair vs. replacement, method, cleanout, and restoration.
* Compare at least two bids.
