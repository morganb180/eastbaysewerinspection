# Selling a home in the East Bay? Get your sewer lateral certificate

In Oakland, Alameda, Albany, Emeryville, Piedmont, El Cerrito, Kensington and Richmond Annex, an EBMUD private sewer lateral **Compliance Certificate is required to close escrow**. Berkeley has its own program.

## The process in brief

| Step | Who |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Check the property's status | Seller, on [eastbaypsl.com](https://www.eastbaypsl.com/) |
| 2. Inspect and repair or replace the lateral, with city permits | Licensed contractor |
| 3. Verification test witnessed by EBMUD ($407 certificate fee) | Contractor + EBMUD |
| 4. Certificate issued; give it to escrow | EBMUD → seller |
| Need more time? Time Extension Certificate ($142 + $4,500 refundable deposit) | Buyer or seller |
