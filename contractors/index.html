Finding a sewer lateral contractor

No listings yet, and we're saying so on purpose. EBMUD provides a courtesy list of contractors (ContractorList.pdf, linked from ebmud.com). Use that list as a starting point; we only publish names we can verify. Contractors: list your business. We'll check your California contractor license (CSLB) before listing.

How do I vet a sewer lateral contractor?

Check their California contractor licence with the CSLB, ask whether they set up the EBMUD verification test and pull the city permit, get the CCTV video and a written scope, and compare at least two bids.

  • Check their California contractor license on the CSLB website. Lateral work is commonly done by plumbing (C-36) or general engineering (A) contractors.
  • Ask whether they schedule and set up the EBMUD verification test and pull the city permit.
  • Get the CCTV video and a written scope: repair vs. replacement, method, cleanout, and restoration.
  • Compare at least two bids.

Which East Bay cities require a sewer lateral certificate?

Oakland, Alameda, Albany, Emeryville, Piedmont, El Cerrito, Kensington and Richmond Annex are in the EBMUD Regional PSL program. Berkeley runs its own separate program.
