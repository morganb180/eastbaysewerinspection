# Sewer lateral certificate when selling in Albany

**Quick answer:** **Do I need a sewer lateral certificate to sell in Albany?** Yes, if the property is an EBMUD wastewater customer in Albany. Albany is covered by the EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance for escrows opened on or after January 1, 2015, so a Compliance Certificate (or an exemption or Time Extension Certificate) is needed to close escrow. A contractor repairs or replaces the lateral, then EBMUD witnesses a verification (leak) test. A CCTV video alone is not enough.

Albany is covered by the EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance. It applies to escrows opened on or after **January 1, 2015**. Only EBMUD wastewater customers in the program area are covered, so check your parcel with EBMUD.

* **Trigger:** a Compliance Certificate is **required to close escrow** when buying or selling, and also for building or remodeling over $100,000 (at final permit sign-off) or changing water meter size ([EBMUD](https://www.ebmud.com/wastewater/private-sewer-laterals)).
* **Test, not video:** a contractor usually runs a CCTV inspection and repairs or replaces the lateral, then sets up a verification (leak) test witnessed by EBMUD. A CCTV video alone is not enough.
* **Need more time?** A Time Extension Certificate gives 180 days after issue to finish the work. It needs a fee plus a **$4,500 refundable deposit**.
* **Exemptions** include properties on septic, fully pressurized laterals, no connected lateral, title transfers not subject to the ordinance, and valid Alameda/Albany certificates.
* **HOAs/condos** had to certify laterals they maintain by July 12, 2021. Buyers should ask the HOA for its Statement of Responsibility.
* **Appeals** within 30 calendar days.

Local permits: Albany may require its own sewer or plumbing permit for lateral repair or replacement. Check with the city.

## Getting the certificate in Albany

1. Check the property's PSL status on [eastbaypsl.com](https://www.eastbaypsl.com/).
2. Get written bids from two or three contractors on EBMUD's list (above, or [all 41](/contractors/)).
3. Your contractor inspects, repairs or replaces the lateral, pulls any city permit and schedules the EBMUD-witnessed verification test.
4. Pass the test, download the Compliance Certificate and give it to escrow.
