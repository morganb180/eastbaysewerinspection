Sewer lateral certificate when selling in Richmond Annex

Quick answer: Do I need a sewer lateral certificate to sell in Richmond Annex? Yes, if the property is an EBMUD wastewater customer in Richmond Annex. Richmond Annex is covered by the EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance through the Stege Sanitary District for escrows opened on or after October 17, 2011, so a Compliance Certificate (or an exemption or Time Extension Certificate) is needed to close escrow. A contractor repairs or replaces the lateral, then EBMUD witnesses a verification (leak) test. A CCTV video alone is not enough.

Richmond Annex is covered by the EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance through the Stege Sanitary District. It applies to escrows opened on or after October 17, 2011 (Stege Sanitary District). Only EBMUD wastewater customers in the program area are covered, so check your parcel with EBMUD.

  • Trigger: a Compliance Certificate is required to close escrow when buying or selling, and also for building or remodeling over $100,000 (at final permit sign-off) or changing water meter size (EBMUD).
  • Test, not video: a contractor usually runs a CCTV inspection and repairs or replaces the lateral, then sets up a verification (leak) test witnessed by EBMUD. A CCTV video alone is not enough.
  • Need more time? A Time Extension Certificate gives 180 days after issue to finish the work. It needs a fee plus a $4,500 refundable deposit.
  • Exemptions include properties on septic, fully pressurized laterals, no connected lateral, title transfers not subject to the ordinance, and valid Alameda/Albany certificates.
  • HOAs/condos had to certify laterals they maintain by July 12, 2021. Buyers should ask the HOA for its Statement of Responsibility.
  • Appeals within 30 calendar days.

Local permits: Richmond Annex may require its own sewer or plumbing permit for lateral repair or replacement. Check with the Stege Sanitary District or the city.

Which contractors on EBMUD's list are based in Richmond Annex?

None of the 41 contractors we list from EBMUD's PSL contractor list (August 2026) has a business address in Richmond Annex. EBMUD doesn't publish service areas, so contractors based in nearby cities may work here: see all 41.

EBMUD publishes this as a courtesy list: contractors volunteered to be on it and self-certified the services they provide. It is not an exclusive list and EBMUD does not endorse anyone. Next step

Getting the certificate in Richmond Annex

  1. Check the property's PSL status on eastbaypsl.com.
  2. Get written bids from two or three contractors on EBMUD's list (above, or all 41).
  3. Your contractor inspects, repairs or replaces the lateral, pulls any city permit and schedules the EBMUD-witnessed verification test.
  4. Pass the test, download the Compliance Certificate and give it to escrow.
