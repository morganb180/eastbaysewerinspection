# List, claim, correct or remove a listing

Every listing on East Bay Sewer Inspection comes from EBMUD's PSL contractor list, checked against your CSLB license record. Use this form to claim your free listing, fix an error, ask to be added, or ask for a listing to be removed. Listings show the business name, mailing address, services and CSLB license. They do not show a phone number, email or website.

## What happens next

1. We check the request against the CSLB record. For claims and corrections we confirm with the contact on the CSLB record, or one you can show is yours.
2. Approved changes go live with the next site update, usually within 5 business days.
3. **Removal:** we remove personal details (for example a home address or personal phone) on request. If you are no longer licensed or certified, tell us and we'll drop the listing. Because listings mirror a public record, we may keep the business name and license status while it stays on the official list.

Listings are free and ordered by location and name, never by payment.
